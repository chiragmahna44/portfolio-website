Case study: growing collaborations for a customer success executive

The client

A customer success executive who wanted to connect with the right people in his market, mainly B2B SaaS companies. The aim was to land as many collaborations as possible and strengthen his name in customer success, with good engagement on LinkedIn along the way.

The challenge

He was enrolled in two or three professional communities, but time was the main issue. He did not have the time to network or start conversations with the people in them, so I did it on his behalf.

What I did

  • Started networking inside his existing community memberships, joining discussions and building relationships with the people active there.
  • Took those same conversations to LinkedIn and reached out to the people I had connected with.
  • Engaged with the right people in his market through thoughtful comments, to keep his profile visible to the right audience.

The result

3 to 4 calls booked within the first 8 days, along with stronger impressions and engagement on his LinkedIn activity.
